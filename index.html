Why it matters

Forms should adapt to people.

Traditional forms can make everyday identity and communication harder than they need to be. LIMITLESS is designed around the person first.

Problems LIMITLESS tackles

Rigid digital forms can struggle when real human information does not fit a predefined template.

×Names that do not naturally fit a First Name / Last Name split
×Addresses that rely on landmarks instead of postal codes
×Code-switching, transliteration, Arabizi, and phonetic spelling
×People having to change how they communicate just to pass validation

What LIMITLESS brings

A more flexible input experience that helps digital systems understand natural human input.

✓Natural input instead of one “correct” format
✓Adaptive understanding of names, addresses, and language
✓Voice input for people who prefer speaking over typing
✓Human control over the final interpreted information
